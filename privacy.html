Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Affiliate Content Bot (the “Application”), operated by Panumart Mala, handles information when you use the Application.

1. Information processed

Depending on the features you use and permissions you approve, the Application may process:

  • TikTok account identifiers and basic profile information returned by TikTok’s authorization service;
  • access tokens, refresh tokens, expiration information, and granted scopes;
  • product names, prices, descriptions, affiliate links, images, captions, audio, videos, schedules, and publishing status that you provide or generate; and
  • technical error messages required to diagnose operations on your device.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to create and preview content, remember your settings, authenticate the account you choose, upload or publish content at your direction, display publishing status, and troubleshoot errors.

3. Storage and processing

Application projects and settings are primarily processed and stored on the user’s Windows device. Where available, sensitive tokens and secrets are stored using Windows Credential Manager. Requests needed for authorization and publishing are sent directly to the relevant official service, such as TikTok.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for third-party advertising. Information is shared with a connected service only when necessary to perform an action you request, such as authorizing an account or publishing a selected video. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Tracking and analytics

This website does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. The Application does not track users across third-party services.

6. Retention and deletion

Local content and settings remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall and remove the Application’s data. You may disconnect an account in the Application and revoke authorization in the connected platform’s settings. Revocation prevents future access; you may also delete locally stored project files and credentials.

7. Security

Reasonable local security measures are used, but no system is completely secure. Protect your Windows account and do not share client secrets or tokens.

8. Children

The Application is not directed to children under 13 and is intended for operators who are legally able to manage and publish promotional content.

9. Changes

This Policy may be updated when features or legal requirements change. The latest version and effective date will be posted on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion assistance, contact Panumart Mala through the developer’s GitHub profile.
